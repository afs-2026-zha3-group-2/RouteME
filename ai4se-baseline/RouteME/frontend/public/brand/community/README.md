# RouteMe 路觅 — community marks

Three marks that put **people** into the logo instead of only the route. Same badge,
gradient, white ink and coral accent as the primary mark; only the composition
changes. Compare them in `community.png`, and see them next to the wordmark in
`community-lockups.png`.

Files per concept: `community-<name>-mark.png` (1024×1024, transparent corners) and
`community-<name>-lockup.png` (mark + wordmark).

---

## A · Shared route — `community-a-shared-route-*`

Two paths run side by side — one white, one coral — and merge into a single
destination pin. You are not travelling alone: someone else is on the same road,
heading to the same place, and the two routes become one at the pin.

The coral path tucks behind the pin so the destination stays clean, and both paths
narrow as they arrive so they flow in rather than collide.

**Strength:** the clearest "travel together" story, and it still reads as a route +
pin at a glance. **Risk:** at favicon size the two paths soften into one thicker
stroke.

**Best for:** the primary logo if community is the headline message — landing page,
pitch deck, app icon.

## B · Circle of people — `community-b-circle-of-people-*`

A thin white ring with six coral dots around a pin at the centre. A group gathered
around the same destination; the ring is the community, the pin is the place.

**Strength:** the most explicit "many people, one place", and visually the most
distinctive of the three at medium sizes. **Risk:** the busiest shape — the dots are
the first thing to blur below ~32 px, and the ring can read as a badge/medal rather
than a map.

**Best for:** community and social sections, event pages, stickers, merch — places
where the mark is shown large.

## C · Team route — `community-c-team-route-*`

Three coral dots sit at the start of a single route that ends in the pin: a small
group setting off together toward the destination.

**Strength:** the simplest of the three, so it holds up best when small, and it
keeps the route + pin reading fully intact. **Risk:** the "group" needs the caption
to be understood as people rather than waypoints.

**Best for:** the most versatile option — works as an app icon, a favicon, and a
nav-bar mark without losing anything.

---

## Choosing

| Need | Pick |
| --- | --- |
| Strongest "we travel together" story | **A** shared route |
| Most explicitly social / community-led | **B** circle of people |
| Safest at small sizes, keeps the original route+pin read | **C** team route |

All three were rendered in the Lagoon colourway. They re-render cleanly in any of the
six palettes in [`../colors/`](../colors/) — the accent colour is the only thing that
changes, so e.g. the Sunset palette turns the coral paths rose and the badge orange.
