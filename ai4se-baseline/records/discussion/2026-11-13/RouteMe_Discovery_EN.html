02

User Persona

Primary Persona

Name / Role

Alex

University Student · Independent Traveler

“I already know where I want to go. I just want to find someone suitable to travel with and see what other people actually did there.”

Background / Context

  • 20–24 years old and travels during weekends, holidays and semester breaks
  • Prefers independent travel to traditional group tours
  • Uses social media, map apps and travel platforms to collect information
  • Has a limited budget and often cannot align schedules with friends

Goals / Needs

  • Find people with the same destination and similar dates
  • Evaluate travel interests and compatibility before connecting
  • Discover authentic experiences instead of generic guides
  • Publish a plan and attract suitable companions
  • Share routes, photos and tips after the trip

Pains / Frustrations

  • Friends are not always available
  • Strangers are difficult to trust
  • Information is fragmented across platforms
  • Commercial and repetitive content creates overload
  • Planning alone takes too much time

Motivations

  • Meet new people through shared travel interests
  • Reduce the cost and effort of travelling alone
  • Make travel more social and memorable
  • Help future travelers through real personal experience
